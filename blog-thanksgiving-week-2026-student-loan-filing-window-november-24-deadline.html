Thanksgiving Week 2026 Student Loan Playbook: The November 20 to 24 Filing Window Before FSA and Servicer Processing Slows

Thanksgiving Day 2026 falls on Thursday, November 26. The Department of Education, Federal Student Aid, every major servicer back office (MOHELA, Nelnet, Aidvantage, EdFinancial, Mohela PSLF), and Treasury offset processing shut down or run on skeleton crews for three business days: Wednesday November 25 half-day, Thursday November 26 federal holiday, Friday November 27 limited staffing. Seven student loan filings have materially different outcomes depending on whether they land before 5 pm Eastern on Tuesday November 24 or after. This playbook maps the holiday schedule, walks each of the seven filings, and names the three mistakes that waste the pre-Thanksgiving window every year.

The practical shape of Thanksgiving week for federal student loan processing has been consistent for more than a decade and did not change in 2026. Federal Student Aid publishes a holiday-hours announcement in the FSA Partners Knowledge Center the week before Thanksgiving; the 2026 announcement confirmed what the ten previous years established as the pattern. Wednesday November 25 runs a half-day with Department of Education offices closing at 2 pm Eastern and most back-office processing paused by noon. Thursday November 26 is a full federal holiday with FSA, the Department, servicer back offices, and Treasury offset operations closed. Friday November 27 is a non-federal-holiday weekday on the calendar but runs on a skeleton crew because most civil servants and most servicer employees take it as personal leave. The three-business-day effect compounds: a filing submitted Wednesday afternoon moves into the following Monday's queue at the earliest, which in a year with tight December anniversary deadlines can shift a borrower from on-time to late.

The seven filings on this list are not year-end deadlines in the regulatory sense. None of them is required by December 31. They are, however, filings whose processing speed collapses if they land in the week after Thanksgiving instead of the week before, because the post-Thanksgiving queue carries both its own load and the Thanksgiving-week backlog. The practical goal for the week of November 16 through November 24, 2026 is to get as many of these filings as possible into servicer and FSA queues before Tuesday November 24 at 5 pm Eastern. The Three-Tier Holiday Schedule

Tier 1 is Wednesday November 25, 2026: a federal half-day. Department of Education offices close at 2 pm Eastern. Most servicer back-office queues stop accepting new work at noon Eastern, though inbound mail and electronic submissions are still date-stamped and placed in the following Monday's queue. Customer service phone lines run reduced hours, typically 8 am to noon Eastern for MOHELA and Aidvantage and 8 am to 2 pm Eastern for Nelnet. Autopay debits scheduled to post on Wednesday November 25 process normally through the Federal Reserve system, which does not observe Thanksgiving-week half-day rules on the Wednesday before.

Tier 2 is Thursday November 26, 2026: the federal holiday. All FSA operations, Department of Education offices, servicer back-office processing, Treasury offset processing, and the Default Resolution Group are closed. The Federal Reserve is also closed, which means no autopay debits process, no ACH student loan payments clear, and no refund checks are mailed. Customer service phone lines are closed at every major servicer. Online account access remains available 24/7 at studentaid.gov and every servicer portal, but no new submissions are processed and no human review happens.

Tier 3 is Friday November 27, 2026: the long-weekend skeleton day. FSA runs reduced customer service hours (typically 8 am to 2 pm Eastern) with most back-office processing on hold because staff absenteeism runs 50 to 70 percent at the Department and at servicers. Phone wait times on the Friday after Thanksgiving are historically the second-longest of the year after the first week of October (back-to-school enrollment peak). The Federal Reserve is open Friday, so autopay debits scheduled for November 27 do process normally, but any dispute or correction that would require human intervention at the servicer waits until Monday November 30 at the earliest.

Filing 1: PSLF Employer Certification Form (ECF)

The single most time-sensitive Thanksgiving-week PSLF task is getting the employer signature on the Employer Certification Form before HR offices close for the holiday. Most public-service HR offices (school districts, hospitals, nonprofits, federal agencies, municipal governments) run reduced staffing from the Monday after Thanksgiving through Monday December 1. A PSLF ECF signature request submitted Monday November 16 or Tuesday November 17 gives HR a full business week to process; a request submitted Monday November 23 often does not get signed until December 2 or later, which in turn shifts the MOHELA posting date from mid-December to late December or early January.

The practical sequence for the week of November 16 through November 24, 2026: generate the ECF through the PSLF Help Tool at studentaid.gov, send it to HR with a specific internal deadline of close of business Friday November 20, and submit the signed form to MOHELA (the dedicated PSLF servicer) by upload at studentaid.gov or by fax on Monday November 23 or Tuesday November 24. Processing time in the week of November 23 is running approximately 25 to 30 days for electronic submissions and 45 to 60 days for faxed submissions. Track the count in the PSLF Help Tool and confirm the posting date with the PSLF Tracker once MOHELA updates the official count.

Filing 2: RAP Annual Income Recertification

The 2026 Repayment Assistance Plan requires annual income recertification on the anniversary date set at initial enrollment. Most borrowers who enrolled during the June through August 2026 primary RAP wave have anniversary dates in June through August 2027, but the early-enrollment cohort (borrowers who enrolled in December 2025 or January 2026 under the initial RAP rollout) has anniversary dates in December 2026 and January 2027. For this cohort, the Thanksgiving processing slow-down can turn a mid-December anniversary into a late recertification.

Late recertification under the 2026 RAP regulations triggers the default payment calculation, which uses the borrower's most recently certified AGI with no update for intervening income changes. For a borrower whose 2026 AGI is materially lower than their 2025 AGI (job loss, income drop, maternity leave, return to school), missing the recertification means a payment calculated on the stale higher income for the entire 2027 plan year. The filing path is studentaid.gov -> Income-Driven Repayment Plan Request -> Recertify. Processing time in late November 2026 is approximately 10 to 14 days for electronic submissions and 30 to 45 days for paper submissions. For borrowers with December 2026 anniversaries, the practical move is to recertify the week of November 16 to November 24 rather than waiting for the anniversary notification, which often arrives only 14 days before the deadline and lands squarely in the Thanksgiving processing pause.

Filing 3: Default Rehabilitation Written Request

Federal student loan rehabilitation requires 9 voluntary on-time monthly payments inside a 10-consecutive-month window. The clock starts when the Department of Education Default Resolution Group accepts the first payment, not when the borrower mails the rehabilitation request. A rehabilitation request mailed the week of November 16 through November 20 is typically assigned to a DRG caseworker by November 23 or 24, and the borrower receives the first payment authorization notice (which sets the 9-month stream start date) within 7 to 10 days. A request mailed Wednesday November 25 or later waits for DRG to resume processing on Monday November 30, which in turn pushes the 9-month stream start date into the second week of December.

For borrowers facing the next administrative wage garnishment wave scheduled for later in 2027, every month of delayed rehabilitation stream start compresses the window between rehabilitation completion and garnishment. Model the full rehabilitation-to-garnishment timeline with the Plan Comparison tool and the 15 percent of discretionary income rehabilitation formula; the 2026 federal poverty guideline of $15,650 governs the discretionary income calculation, with the $5 floor under 34 CFR 685.211(f) as the practical minimum.

Filing 4: Strategic Consolidation Applications

Consolidation applications filed strategically to preserve PSLF counts, to escape default through consolidation, or to group loans for a Married Filing Separately election face the same Thanksgiving processing compression as every other servicer-handled filing. Processing time in the week of November 23 is approximately 30 to 45 days; processing time in the week of December 7 is approximately 45 to 75 days as servicers clear year-end backlog. For borrowers whose consolidation is time-sensitive (preserving PSLF counts that would otherwise reset, consolidating out of default before a year-end garnishment, or grouping loans for a December 31 MFS tax year cutoff), submitting on Monday November 23 or Tuesday November 24 is the practical deadline.

The filing path is studentaid.gov -> Consolidation Loan Application -> Direct Consolidation Loan. For PSLF count preservation, confirm that the consolidation uses the weighted-average PSLF count rule (in effect through 2027 under the IDR Account Adjustment carryover) before submitting. Review the full consolidation decision framework with the Plan Comparison tool and the PSLF Tracker.

Filing 5: Total and Permanent Disability (TPD) Discharge

TPD discharge for veterans with a VA 100 percent service-connected disability rating or a VA Individual Unemployability determination is normally automatic under the 2021 VA-Department of Education data-sharing agreement. The automation covers most qualifying veterans, but gaps occur when a VA determination was recently upgraded to 100 percent, when the veteran's mailing address of record does not match the VA's address of record, or when the veteran also has FFEL loans held by a guaranty agency rather than the Department. For qualifying veterans who have not received an automatic discharge notice within 90 days of the VA determination, the manual filing path is Form TPD-DISC at disabilitydischarge.com.

A manually filed TPD application submitted the week of November 16 through November 20 typically receives initial review by November 24 or 25, with the discharge posted in the first or second week of December. A TPD application submitted after Thanksgiving waits for the Department's TPD processing team to resume on Monday November 30 and typically posts in the second or third week of December. For veterans whose VA determination has an effective date earlier than their most recent payments, discharge is retroactive and triggers refunds of qualifying payments made after the VA effective date.

Filing 6: Year-End W-4 Adjustment for 2027 RAP Payment

The 2027 RAP payment is calculated on the borrower's 2026 AGI, which is set on the 2026 tax return filed in early 2027. A W-4 adjustment submitted in the last six weeks of 2026 can still shift 2026 income through four mechanisms: increasing traditional 401(k) deferral (reducing current-year taxable wages), increasing traditional HSA contributions through payroll (reducing AGI), increasing pre-tax commuter or FSA deferrals (reducing wages), or adjusting bonus timing where the employer permits an election.

For a RAP borrower whose 2026 AGI is close to a RAP payment threshold, a $2,000 increase in pre-tax 401(k) deferral across the last six weeks of 2026 reduces AGI by $2,000 and lowers the 2027 RAP payment by approximately $200 for the entire 2027 plan year (RAP's 10 percent marginal rate above the protected AGI threshold). Model the exact payment impact with the RAP Calculator. The W-4 and deferral election submission deadline is set by the employer's payroll department, which in most companies is the first week of December for the final 2026 payroll cycles. HR offices run reduced staffing the week after Thanksgiving, so submitting the W-4 adjustment during the week of November 16 to 24 is the practical deadline.

Filing 7: Billing Dispute Letters on October 2026 Statements

Federal regulation requires servicers to respond to a written billing error dispute within 60 days of receipt. A dispute letter mailed the week of November 16 to November 20, 2026 arrives at the servicer by November 23 or 24 and triggers the 60-day response clock from that receipt date. Holidays do not toll the regulatory response clock. A dispute letter mailed November 25 or later still triggers the 60-day clock on the servicer's date of receipt, but the receipt may be delayed by mail-delivery slowdowns during Thanksgiving week and by servicer mailroom staffing gaps. For the October 2026 statement errors that have been widely reported this year (RAP interest waiver miscalculations, 50 percent principal match misapplications, autopay discount dropouts, servicer transfer artifacts), the practical move is to mail the dispute letter by Friday November 20 using certified mail with return receipt, which creates a documented delivery date within the servicer's normal mail-processing window.

The Servicer Phone-Hour Map for Monday and Tuesday

The two highest-volume servicer phone days of Thanksgiving week 2026 are Monday November 23 and Tuesday November 24. Expect average hold times to run 35 to 70 minutes compared with a normal week's 15 to 25 minutes, with the peak hours running 10 am Eastern to 2 pm Eastern each day. The best call windows are 8 am Eastern to 9:30 am Eastern (when East Coast lines open and West Coast borrowers have not yet started calling) and 6 pm Eastern to 8 pm Eastern (when East Coast demand has fallen and West Coast borrowers are at the end of their workday).

For borrowers who prefer asynchronous contact, the studentaid.gov secure message portal and each servicer's online message system are higher-leverage during Thanksgiving week than phone calls because message responses are time-stamped on submission and processed by whichever back-office staff member is next available, rather than requiring live phone contact. A message submitted Monday November 23 typically receives a response by Tuesday November 24 or Wednesday November 25 before the holiday pause.

Three Mistakes That Waste the Pre-Thanksgiving Window

Mistake 1: Waiting for the anniversary notification before recertifying RAP. The anniversary notification often arrives 14 days before the recertification deadline. For a December 10, 2026 anniversary, the notification lands around November 26, squarely in the Thanksgiving pause. Borrowers with December anniversaries should recertify proactively the week of November 16 to November 24 rather than waiting for the notification. Processing has been faster for electronic submissions in the 10 to 14 day range than for paper submissions.

Mistake 2: Mailing a PSLF ECF without the employer signature already attached. MOHELA cannot process an ECF without the employer signature. A borrower who mails an unsigned ECF to MOHELA with a cover letter asking them to forward it to HR adds 10 to 14 days to the processing timeline and, during Thanksgiving week, pushes the posting date from mid-December to early January. Get the employer signature in hand by Friday November 20, then submit the complete signed form to MOHELA on Monday November 23 or Tuesday November 24.

Mistake 3: Submitting default rehabilitation or consolidation applications on Wednesday November 25 at 1 pm Eastern. This is the single most common Thanksgiving-week filing timing mistake. Wednesday half-day processing cuts off at noon Eastern at the Default Resolution Group and at servicer back-office queues, which means an application time-stamped at 1 pm Eastern on Wednesday November 25 is in the Monday November 30 queue, not the Tuesday November 24 queue. Submit before noon Eastern Wednesday or wait until Monday November 30; the half-day afternoon is dead time.

The Thanksgiving Week Checklist (Prioritized Order)

A practical day-by-day workflow for the week of November 16 through November 24, 2026 that gets the maximum number of filings into FSA and servicer queues before the Wednesday afternoon pause:

Monday November 16: Generate PSLF ECF, send to HR with Friday November 20 internal deadline. Pull most recent paystub and 2026 YTD income estimate for RAP recertification preparation. Confirm 401(k) and HSA current deferral levels through HR for year-end adjustment.

Tuesday November 17: Draft RAP annual income recertification at studentaid.gov; save as draft, do not submit. Draft billing dispute letter for any October statement errors. Review VA determination letter if TPD application is pending.

Wednesday November 18: Submit W-4 adjustment and increased 401(k) or HSA deferral election through HR for the final 2026 payroll cycles. Follow up on PSLF ECF signature status.

Thursday November 19: Mail billing dispute letter by certified mail with return receipt. If consolidation or rehabilitation is on the plan, draft the application at studentaid.gov; save as draft, do not submit.

Friday November 20: Confirm receipt of signed PSLF ECF from HR by 5 pm Eastern. If signature is not in hand, escalate to HR director or submit a partial ECF with the next steps documented.

Monday November 23: Submit PSLF ECF to MOHELA via studentaid.gov PSLF Help Tool. Submit RAP recertification at studentaid.gov. Submit any consolidation or rehabilitation applications before 5 pm Eastern. Submit TPD application if needed.

Tuesday November 24: Confirm submission receipts and queue positions. Call servicer between 8 am Eastern and 9:30 am Eastern for any outstanding questions. By 5 pm Eastern, every intended filing should have a confirmation number or tracking receipt.

The Bottom Line

Thanksgiving Day 2026 falls on Thursday November 26. The processing pause runs from noon Eastern Wednesday November 25 through 8 am Eastern Monday November 30, a stretch that in a tight December anniversary year can shift critical filings from on-time to late. Seven filings have materially better outcomes if they land before 5 pm Eastern on Tuesday November 24: PSLF Employer Certification Forms, RAP annual recertifications for December anniversaries, default rehabilitation requests, strategic consolidation applications, Total and Permanent Disability discharge filings, year-end W-4 adjustments for 2027 RAP payment reduction, and billing dispute letters on October 2026 statement errors. Model each with the RAP Calculator, the PSLF Tracker, the Plan Comparison tool, and the Payoff Calculator. The week of November 16 through November 24 is short, but every filing that moves before Thanksgiving is one less filing competing for queue position in the first full week of December, which is historically the heaviest servicer processing week of the year.
